Searching only for “robotics engineer” can hide much of the work at a robotics company. Someone has to assemble the hardware, validate sensors, design the interface and help customers use the product. Those jobs often have different titles.

Start with the machines you want to work on. Around Zurich and Lausanne, you can find employers building inspection robots, mapping drones and surgical systems. This guide connects those products to companies in the Job Seek catalogue, with examples of roles we found when checking their listings on 28 September 2026.

<h2 id="zurich" className="scroll-mt-20">Zurich: inspection robots and aerial mapping</h2>

### Industrial inspection on legs

<Company slug="anybotics" /> builds autonomous legged robots for industrial inspection. Its careers page identifies Zurich as a base, but the company also lists jobs elsewhere. Keep the location filter on when browsing its roles.

The Zurich listings we checked included an Industrialization / Electronics Test Engineer and a Production Technician II (Robotics), alongside a software engineering internship. This is a useful employer to explore if your interest is getting a physical robot built, tested and working reliably, as well as writing its software.

### Flying robots that touch the structure

<Company slug="voliro" /> develops aerial robots that make physical contact with structures for inspection. Its Zurich office is a starting point for a different kind of robotics search: flight, inspection tools and the software around them.

Our catalogue check found a Senior Cloud Engineer listing in Zurich. A spontaneous-application listing was also present; it is an invitation to introduce yourself, not a specific open role.

### Drones for surveying and mapping

<Company slug="wingtra" /> develops drone-based surveying and mapping systems, with engineering and manufacturing in Zurich. The product includes the aircraft, sensors and software used to turn a flight into useful mapping data.

Its Zurich listings included Production Operator Assembly, Junior Software Engineer – GTM Products, and Team Lead Procurement & Supply Chain. Read the duties behind each title: a software role at a drone company is not necessarily flight-control engineering.

<h2 id="lausanne" className="scroll-mt-20">Lausanne area: indoor drones and surgical robotics</h2>

Use Lausanne as a regional starting point, then check the actual workplace. Flyability and Distalmotion are in nearby municipalities, not central Lausanne.

### Inspection inside confined spaces

<Company slug="flyability" /> develops indoor inspection drones and software. Its headquarters is in **Paudex**, in the Lausanne area. Think about the whole inspection workflow: collecting data in a difficult space, interpreting it and presenting it clearly to the person doing the inspection.

When we checked, its Vaud listings included Sensor Validation & Calibration Engineer, Senior UX/UI Designer and Machine Learning Engineer – Spatial AI. Open the individual posting to check the workplace, experience requirements and working language.

### Robots for the operating room

<Company slug="distalmotion" /> develops the DEXTER robotic surgery system. Its Swiss address is in **Epalinges**, north of Lausanne. This is a medical-device employer to consider if you want to work on physical systems used in a clinical setting.

Our catalogue included a Robotics Manufacturing Engineer role and a Complaint Handling & PMS Specialist role in Vaud. Here, PMS means post-market surveillance: monitoring a device after it reaches the market. Manufacturing and quality work can be as relevant to a robotics search as controls or perception. Check the requirements for medical-device experience in the specific role.

### More drone employers near Lausanne

- <Company slug="flybotix" /> — indoor inspection drones, based in Renens. Its careers feed includes technical and customer-facing work.
- <Company slug="rigitech" /> — drone delivery systems, based in Prilly. Check commercial and operations roles as well as engineering.

<h2 id="beyond" className="scroll-mt-20">Elsewhere in Switzerland</h2>

### Precision agriculture in Yverdon-les-Bains

<Company slug="ecorobotix" /> develops precision spraying systems that use AI and optics to target plants. It is based in **Yverdon-les-Bains**, elsewhere in Vaud, rather than Lausanne. Include it if that location or agricultural technology fits your search.

Its catalogue page includes overseas roles too. A Swiss headquarters does not make every opening a Swiss job. Check the location of each posting before adding it to your application list; a role with no clear location needs confirmation.

### Surgical systems, warehouse automation and unmanned aircraft

- <Company slug="cascination" /> — surgical robotics and image-guided procedures, including the HEARO system, in Bern.
- <Company slug="swisslog" /> — warehouse automation, robotic picking and material handling, with Swiss operations in Buchs **AG**.
- <Company slug="dufour-aerospace" /> — unmanned tilt-wing aircraft, with locations in Dübendorf and Visp.
- <Company slug="swissdrones" /> — long-range unmanned helicopters, with headquarters in Zurich and manufacturing in Buchs **SG**. This is a different Buchs from Swisslog's Aargau location.
- <Company slug="staubli" /> — industrial robots and mechatronics, with Swiss operations including Horgen. Its wider business also covers connectors and textile machinery.

<h2 id="methodology" className="scroll-mt-20">How we chose these employers</h2>

We checked the employers’ own product, careers and contact pages, their entries in the Job Seek catalogue, and the roles displayed there on **28 September 2026**. This list covers robot makers, autonomy teams and specialist suppliers with verified career sources. It is not a ranking or a census of every robotics-related business. Locations are named separately so that Paudex, Epalinges, Renens and Prilly are not mistaken for central Lausanne, or Buchs AG for Buchs SG.

Read the original posting before applying, and confirm details it leaves unanswered. An English-language advert alone does not establish the team’s working language.
