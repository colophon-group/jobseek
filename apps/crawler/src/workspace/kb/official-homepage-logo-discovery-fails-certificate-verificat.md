# Official homepage logo discovery fails certificate verification but the confirmed ATS tenant remains reachable.

## Problem
Official homepage logo discovery fails certificate verification but the confirmed ATS tenant remains reachable.

## Solution
Discover logo candidates from the independently verified official careers tenant, visually inspect the generated JPG previews, select the official branding, then restore the canonical corporate website with --no-discover. For AHMC, iCIMS provided a 556x208 purple cross and wordmark; generic person icons were rejected.
