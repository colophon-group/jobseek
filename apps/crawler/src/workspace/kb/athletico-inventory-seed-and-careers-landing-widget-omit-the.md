# Athletico inventory seed and careers landing widget omit the separate corporate and clinic-support inventory.

## Problem
Athletico inventory seed and careers landing widget omit the separate corporate and clinic-support inventory.

## Solution
Follow each official career category link. Athletico licensed clinical roles use clinicalexternal-athletico.icims.com (439 jobs, 22 pages), while corporate and support use careers-athletico.icims.com (278 jobs, 14 pages). Configure both unfiltered native iCIMS boards; compare overlap and verify audience and requisition identity. Pivot Physical Therapy shares these portals. Treat widget counts as partial until reconciled against complete ATS pagination.
