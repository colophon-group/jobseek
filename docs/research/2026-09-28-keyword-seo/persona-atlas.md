## Deliberate overlaps

These memberships reflect assigned circumstances. They are not prevalence counts and do not mean identical product fit.

## Reading the fit assessments

A high persona-assessed fit refers to a particular task under the scenario. It is not a ranking forecast, purchase probability or final content priority. Use the main report to account for real product boundaries and supply checks. A persona can have both high-fit and poor-fit queries.
